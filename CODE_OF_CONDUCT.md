# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility and apologizing to people affected by mistakes

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discrimination,
sexualized language or imagery, trolling, public or private intimidation, and
publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of
Conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other
contributions that violate it, and may take other actions they consider
appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and when an individual is
officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the
contact method listed in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Please include enough detail
to allow the report to be investigated.
